About me

A curious mind
always exploring.

Hi, I'm Mahi — an MSc Artificial Intelligence student at the University of Roehampton. I'm fascinated by machine learning, intelligent systems, and what happens when you put powerful technology in the hands of creative people.

On the side, I'm a dedicated car spotter. There's something about the engineering, the design, and the sheer presence of a great car that I can't get enough of. This site is where both worlds collide.

Background

Where I'm from

Born and raised in Oman, I grew up surrounded by a culture that values family, community, and ambition. Oman shaped a lot of who I am — the curiosity, the drive, and an eye for the things that stand out.

Moving to London to pursue my MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Roehampton was a big step, but one I knew I had to take. I wanted to be at the intersection of technology and the real world — and that's exactly where I find myself now.

Outside of studying, you'll find me on the streets of London with my camera, spotting cars, or on the Apex Legends servers. Two very different worlds, but both fuelled by the same thing — passion.
