Drone Search & Rescue Live Feed

Search and Rescue Drone

A group project developed during my undergraduate degree — a search and rescue drone equipped with a live camera feed accessible through a custom web interface. Designed to assist emergency teams by streaming real-time aerial footage to a browser, removing the need for specialised hardware on the ground.

The project was presented at the Plymouth Marine Laboratory, where we demonstrated the drone's live feed capabilities and its potential applications in coastal and maritime search and rescue operations.
